How it works

Three steps. Zero opinions.

You bring the prop line the sportsbook is offering. PropBetIQ tells you how often it's actually hit.

01
Search your player
Type any player name or team code. Get every stat category they play — passing, rushing, receiving, defensive props, points, assists, rebounds, and every combo bet market.
02
Enter the line
Enter the exact O/U line your sportsbook is offering — or slide through any threshold. Hit rate updates live. Sample size shown alongside so you know if it's real signal or small-N noise.
03
Filter by context
Stack filters for home/away, weather, opponent strength, Q1 pace, recent form. Watch every panel recompute against the exact scenario you're betting on. That's the edge sportsbooks aren't pricing in.
